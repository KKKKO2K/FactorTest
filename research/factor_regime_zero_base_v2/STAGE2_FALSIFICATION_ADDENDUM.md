# Stage 2 Falsification Addendum — Non-Overlapping Sampling

Written before Stage-2 results are run.

Because the 5D state grid creates overlapping 20D current/future observations across adjacent signal dates, Stage 2 also includes a non-overlapping robustness test.

For each universe, use every fourth eligible 5D signal date and recompute the primary current-20D vs future-20D dispersion IC by era.

For the opportunity-clustering relation to be classified HISTORICALLY ROBUST, the median non-overlapping IC across universes must be positive in both 2020–22 and 2023–24, in addition to the gates in STAGE2_FALSIFICATION.md.
