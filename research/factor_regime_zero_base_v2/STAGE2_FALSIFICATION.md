# Stage 2 Pre-Registered Falsification — Factor Opportunity Clustering

Written after Stage 1 and before Stage-2 results are observed.

## What survives Stage 1

Only one phenomenon proceeds:

**Factor opportunity clustering** — current cross-factor payoff magnitude predicts future cross-factor payoff magnitude.

Three Stage-1 predictors passed, but they are treated as alternative measurements of the same latent object, not three independent signals:
- factor range;
- factor dispersion;
- mean absolute factor payoff.

The primary measurement for Stage 2 is **20D cross-factor dispersion** because it is symmetric and less dependent on one extreme factor than range.

Cross-universe coherence prediction is stopped: no pre-registered Stage-1 coherence predictor passed.

Winner continuation remains stopped because Stage-0 top-factor survival was near its random-overlap baseline.

## Falsification tests

### A. Horizon robustness
Test current 20D dispersion against forward dispersion over:
- 10D;
- 20D;
- 40D;
- 60D.

No horizon is selected after seeing results. 20D remains primary.

### B. Gap robustness
Repeat the 20D target after skipping the immediately following 5D block. This tests whether the relation is only a boundary/very-short-memory effect.

### C. Leave-one-factor-out
For each of the 8 canonical factors, recompute current and future 20D dispersion using the other 7 factors. The relation should not depend on one factor family.

### D. Volatility normalization
Normalize both current and future 20D dispersion by the median trailing-60D 5D volatility across factors, using only information available at t for the denominator. This tests whether the result is merely generic factor-volatility clustering.

### E. Cross-universe aggregation
At each date, take the median current and future factor dispersion across the six universes and test the time-series relation. This checks whether the phenomenon is common rather than an artifact of one universe definition.

### F. Era stability
Report separately:
- 2016–2019;
- 2020–2022;
- 2023–2024;
- 2025;
- 2026.

2025/2026 remain stress evidence and cannot change the historical robustness definition.

## Robustness classification

The primary opportunity-clustering relation is **HISTORICALLY ROBUST** only if all are true:
1. primary 20D current-to-future dispersion IC is positive in the median universe in 2016–19, 2020–22 and 2023–24;
2. at least 4/6 universes have positive primary IC in both 2020–22 and 2023–24;
3. gap-5D robustness has positive median IC in both 2020–22 and 2023–24;
4. volatility-normalized robustness has positive median IC in both periods;
5. at least 7/8 leave-one-factor-out variants have positive median IC in both periods;
6. cross-universe aggregate current/future dispersion IC is positive in both periods.

If the above passes but 2025 and/or 2026 are negative, classify the result as **HISTORICALLY ROBUST / RECENT BREAK**, not as a production signal.

No portfolio test is allowed until this falsification stage is complete.
